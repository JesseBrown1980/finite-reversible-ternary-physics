## Critical correction: the published “constant-density thermal slab” has no initial thermal gradient

The source initializes every cell to ones, and in both hot and cold halves sets the rest channel to 2 and channel 1 (an edge velocity) to 2. It then selects either face occupancies `(2,2,0,0,0,0)` or `(0,0,1,1,1,1)`.

Recomputing exact moments from the source at tick 0 gives:

| Local quantity | “Hot” | “Cold” |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total occupancy / density | 27 | 27 |
| Rest count | 2 | 2 |
| Face count | 4 | 4 |
| Edge count | 13 | 13 |
| Corner count | 8 | 8 |
| Microscopic energy `sum n_k |c_k|²` | **54** | **54** |
| Local momentum | **(-1,-1,0)** | **(-1,-1,0)** |

The difference is in **directional face occupancy and stress**, not density, bulk momentum, or energy. The 100% initial active-collision result is reproducible but does not establish heat transfer driven by a pre-existing temperature difference.

The independently replayed 48-tick evolution of that *published* initialization has:

| Tick | Mean energy, labeled “hot” half | Mean energy, labeled “cold” half | Density range |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 54.0000 | 54.0000 | 27–27 |
| 1 | 54.3333 | 53.6667 | 25–29 |
| 6 | 54.5833 | 53.4167 | 23–31 |
| 12 | 54.5833 | 53.4167 | 23–33 |
| 48 | 54.4167 | 53.5833 | 23–35 |

Hence an *energy* contrast is **generated by the evolution** from an initially equal-energy, differently oriented state. The observed behavior is a useful anisotropic-stress-to-energy redistribution experiment, not yet a thermal-conduction benchmark.

## New independent corrected heat control

I constructed a state with exactly **27 occupied particles per cell, zero momentum per cell**, but unequal starting energies, without changing the microscopic update:

- High-energy half: set opposite face channels `(22, 4)` from 1 to 0 and opposite edge channels `(1, 25)` from 1 to 2; gives `E=56`, `rho=27`, `P=(0,0,0)`.
- Low-energy half: reverse those changes; gives `E=52`, `rho=27`, `P=(0,0,0)`.

The resulting time series is supplied in `corrected_thermal_timeseries.csv`.

| Tick | High-energy half | Low-energy half | Difference |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 56.0000 | 52.0000 | +4.0000 |
| 1 | 55.0000 | 53.0000 | +2.0000 |
| 6 | 52.6667 | 55.3333 | -2.6667 |

| 24 | 57.3333 | 50.6667 | +6.6667 |

This is **not monotonic Fourier heat diffusion**. It is consistent with strong reversible wave-like/directional coupling and density generation (by tick 48, mean half-densities are 31 and 23). A proper constitutive test needs several wavelengths, a macroscopic heat-flux observer, controlled ensemble means, and independent damped-wave/diffusion fits.

## Remaining gaps

1. **Main suite not migrated:** `asolaria_equilibrium_suite.cpp` has the same Git blob hash as before (`d44fa88804c20ef03290cfb32eba5811577193f4`); the previous acoustic, Taylor–Green and heat CSVs are unchanged. They therefore describe the previous 288-gate collision system, not this new cross-shell update.
2. **Physical Host8 unattested here:** the physical 300GB disk was unavailable; the published SHA256 values remain provenance claims rather than independently replayed disk measurements.
3. **Macroscopic physics uncalibrated:** exact particle and energy redistribution does not by itself determine Fourier conductivity, viscosity, or continuum rotational isotropy.
4. **Harness improvement:** new inter-shell code uses `assert` for several invariants; in `-DNDEBUG` builds these assertions disappear. Explicit always-on fail-fast conditions would be stronger for a published acceptance test.

## Verdict

**PASS for the new mathematical/computational claim**: a nontrivial inter-shell collision update exists that is conserved, microscopically reversible through a specified inverse, and equivariant under the full 48-operation cubic group. It reproduces precisely the reported random activation count and full four-shell 48-tick trajectory.

**NOT YET established:** an initial thermal-energy gradient in the published slab, a verified constitutive Fourier law for the new operator, or the previously published full macroscopic benchmarks using the new operator.

Recommended next commit: correct the thermal initial condition and name the old state an *anisotropic-stress control*, then integrate the cross-shell kernel into the actual main physics suite and repeat multi-seed, multi-wavenumber transport measurements before making thermal-conduction claims.
